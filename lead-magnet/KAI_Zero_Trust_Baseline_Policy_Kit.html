Questions before you start

  • Who looks after Microsoft 365 and agrees changes?
  • Which Microsoft subscriptions and features do you have?
  • Do you know which accounts, devices, apps and sensitive files you have?
  • How will you test changes, keep emergency access and tell the team?
  • Who checks that the rules work and follows up on alerts?

People & accounts

Confirm who is requesting access and keep account access current.

Who can sign in? Who has admin access? How do you remove access when someone leaves? Make sure you can regain access in an emergency. Accounts used by apps need different checks from people.

  • PO1: Protect admin sign-in from fake websites
  • PO2: Emergency access if normal sign-in fails
  • PO3: A second sign-in check for everyone
  • PO4: A second sign-in check for guests
  • PO5: Protect how sign-in methods and devices are added
  • PO6: Limit who can invite guests
  • PR1: Remove admin access people no longer need
  • PR2: Update access when people join, change roles or leave

Devices

Check whether devices are safe enough to access work information.

Which devices can access work? Who checks updates and protection? What happens if a device is lost?

  • PO12: Block device types you do not support
  • PO13: Require computers to pass security checks
  • PO14: Use approved apps on mobile devices
  • PO15: Turn on Microsoft device protection
  • PO16: Protect stored files if a device is lost
  • PO17: Keep devices updated automatically
  • PO18: Change local admin passwords automatically

Access rules

Decide when to allow access, limit it or block it.

Which sign-in rules fit your team? Check available Microsoft features and test emergency access before making changes.

  • PO7: Block older, less secure sign-in methods
  • PO8: Block sign-in through device codes
  • PO9: Block moving a sign-in between devices
  • PO10: Set how long a sign-in lasts
  • PO11: Avoid staying signed in after closing the browser

Email & message protection

Help keep harmful messages and fake senders away from your team.

Who looks after email protection? Are messages from your business easy to verify? Who follows up on suspicious messages?

  • PO19: Protect email and Teams from harmful content
  • PO20: Help stop others pretending to email from your business

Files & sharing

Help people share work information with the right people.

Which information is sensitive? Who can share it outside your business? Agree those rules before changing file settings.

  • PR4: Agree which information needs extra protection
  • PO22: Set safer sharing defaults
  • PO23: Mark files and email by how sensitive they are
  • PO24: Help prevent sensitive information being shared
  • PO25: Block automatic forwarding to outside email addresses

Apps & AI

Know which apps and AI tools can use business information.

Which apps and AI tools use your business information? Who approves them? What can they see or change? Different tools may need different settings.

  • PO21: Approve connected apps and AI tools before use
  • PR3: Keep a list of connected apps and AI tools

Activity & alerts

See what happened and make sure someone follows up.

What activity do you record, and for how long? Does your subscription support it? Who receives alerts and follows up?

  • PO26: Record important activity
  • PO27: Keep sign-in records for 90 days
  • PO28: Send alerts for important security events
  • PR5: Assign someone to act on alerts

Owners & reviews

Give each rule an owner and keep it useful as your business changes.

Who owns each policy? How are exceptions agreed? When will you check that the rules still fit your business?

  • PR6: Assign policy owners and record agreed exceptions
  • PR7: Review Microsoft security recommendations monthly
  • PR8: Review the policies every three months
