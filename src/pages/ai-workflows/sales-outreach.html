Sales Outreach questions.

Does the AI send messages to prospects on its own? +

No. AI researches leads and drafts outreach, but a person on your sales team reviews the data, message and recipient list before anything is sent. Automation only sends approved content, following the rules you agree.

Where do the leads come from? +

From sources you already have: your CRM, sign-up forms or company prospect lists. The AI checks sources, assesses fit against your criteria and flags missing information rather than guessing.

Which channels can it send through? +

Email or direct messages through channels you connect and approve. KAI sets which channels each agent can use and the sending rules it must follow.

How do we know it is working? +

Measure qualified leads, positive replies, meetings booked and time spent preparing each lead, and compare them with how the team worked before the pilot.
