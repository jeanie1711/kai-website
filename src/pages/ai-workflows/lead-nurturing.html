AI workflow for marketing

Lead Nurturing. Stay in touch until they are ready.

Lead Nurturing is an AI workflow that drafts content for each audience segment, sends it after your team reviews it, and tracks engagement. It keeps you in touch with leads who are not ready to buy, without adding hours to your marketing week.

Lead Nurturing questions.

Will AI-written content sound like our brand? +

The AI drafts from your offer, tone and past examples, and a person reviews every piece before it is sent. Edits from reviewers help the drafts improve over time.

How is this different from Sales Outreach? +

Sales Outreach prepares one-to-one messages for leads your team is actively pursuing. Lead Nurturing keeps a wider audience segment engaged over time, so leads who are not ready to buy yet remember you when they are.

What do we need to start? +

A list of contacts you are allowed to market to, a sending channel such as your email platform, and a clear idea of one audience segment to start with.
