Documents & Follow-ups. Less retyping, fewer dropped tasks.

Documents & Follow-ups is an AI workflow that reads incoming emails and documents, such as invoices, requests or meeting notes, extracts the details and routes the task to the right person after a human review. It reduces manual entry, handoffs and repeat questions.

Documents & Follow-ups questions.

Which emails and documents can the workflow handle? +

Routine items with a predictable set of details, such as supplier invoices, purchase orders, standard forms, customer requests and meeting notes. We start with one type and add others once the first is working well.

Does the AI read all of our email? +

No. It works only with the inboxes, folders or notes you agree on. Personal mailboxes and other channels stay outside the workflow.

Will it pay invoices or make commitments automatically? +

No. The workflow prepares the details and routes the task. Payments, approvals and commitments stay with named people, and unclear owners or deadlines are confirmed by a person first.

What about poor scans or unclear requests? +

Details the AI is unsure about are flagged for a person to check rather than guessed. The pilot measures how many corrections are needed, so you can see whether the workflow is accurate enough to keep.
