Case study · Zero Trust Security Foundation

Protecting customer data wherever the sales team works, or shares it.

A financial solutions consultancy wanted its sales team to move fast, use familiar chat apps and lean on AI for daily work. The task was to protect customer information without getting in the team’s way.

Industry: Financial solutions consultancyTeam: Mobile sales teamService: KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation
Overview

Access that follows the person and the device, not the folder.

The company built a practical security model where the right to open sensitive information depends on who is asking, which device they use and the company’s own rules, not on where a file happens to be stored or shared.

The result in one line

Information could keep moving through chat apps, personal phones and AI tools, while the right to open it stayed under the company’s control.

The challenge

A mobile team, familiar tools and data on the move.

The sales team worked from many locations, often on personal laptops, phones and tablets, and reached customers through Zalo, WhatsApp, Viber and other messaging apps. Prospect lists, customer details and deal notes moved across channels, devices and networks. That flexibility mattered, but it created three real risks.

Leavers could keep access

Sales turnover was high. People who left might still sit in old chat groups or hold downloaded files. Removing them from company systems didn’t remove the copies they had.

Sensitive files on personal devices

Salespeople needed customer and deal information on the road, so sensitive documents were opened on devices the company didn’t fully control.

Customer data in personal AI

The team used tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to draft proposals and follow-ups. Customer and deal details sometimes went into personal AI accounts with no clear rules.

The solution

Protect the data, not the channel.

The company didn’t lock its sales team into one app, device or network. Instead of controlling where information was shared, the KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation shifted the focus to who could open it, combining clear rules, practical controls and simple guidance for employees.

Sensitive files were labelled and protected

Sales information was grouped by sensitivity, and each group carried its own rules: who can open it, on which devices, whether it can be copied, printed or shared, and when access expires. The protection travelled with the file.

Internal: general sales materialsConfidential: prospect lists, pricing, pipeline, deal notesHighly confidential: customer personal and financial records

Sharing a file no longer meant giving away access

People could still send documents through any chat app, but a protected file only opened for someone signed in with an approved company account, authorised for that information, on a device that met company requirements. Forward it to an outsider and they could receive it, but not read it.

Former employees could no longer open retained files

When a salesperson left, their account and access were removed. Even with an old chat group, a downloaded copy or a re-sent file, the protected document no longer opened. The company stopped relying on someone remembering to clear every group and folder.

Personal devices stayed usable

Nobody was banned from personal devices. What you could open depended on sensitivity: general materials on most devices, confidential documents only on approved secure devices, and the most sensitive files blocked from copying, printing or downloading. Company data could be removed from a device without touching personal information.

AI stayed useful, under clear rules

The company set simple everyday rules for AI in sales work, with practical examples, and where possible stopped sensitive files from being uploaded into unapproved AI tools.

SafeDraft outlines, improve wording, prepare meeting questions, use public product information
After removing detailsSummarise requirements or draft from a scenario, once names and identifying details are removed
Not allowedUpload customer financials, paste full prospect lists or pricing
