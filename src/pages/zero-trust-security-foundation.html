A few things you may want to know

Before you get started.

Using AI as well? See how KAI applies the same Zero Trust rules to AI agents and workflows.

Is this for a business like ours? +

The kit is designed for businesses with around 20–200 people using Microsoft 365. You can review it with your business owner, operations lead or the person who looks after your IT.

Do we need to buy more Microsoft subscriptions? +

Some policies may use features you already pay for. Others may need a different subscription or an extra feature. KAI checks what you have before recommending changes.

Will the changes make work harder for our team? +

Some rules change how people sign in, use devices or share files. We test the changes, introduce them step by step and help your team understand what to do.

Can we use the free kit without hiring KAI? +

Yes. Review the policies with your IT team or IT partner and decide what fits your business. The kit gives you the policy list and questions to check. You will still need someone who can safely set up and test the changes.

Does marking the map confirm we are secure? +

No. The marks are your own notes about what to check. They do not test your systems. Your notes stay in this browser and can be included in your download.

What do we receive when we work with KAI? +

You receive the policies agreed for your business, records of the setup and tests, guidance for your team and suggested next steps. These are brought together in your KAI Zero Trust Security Pack.
