What it is

Three parts that only work when they work together.

One outcome: your business-critical information protected as you adopt AI, using the Microsoft 365 and Entra controls you already pay for.

Policy Design

The rules. Plain-language guidelines for who, and what (including AI tools), can reach your information, written around how your business actually works. Never a template.

Technical Implementation

The setup. We configure Microsoft 365 and Entra controls for sign-in, access, devices and data, so the rules are enforced rather than just written down.

Team Adoption (ACM)

The behaviour. Communication, training and reinforcement that turn new rules into everyday habits. The engagement is only done when people keep using them.

Team adoption is what sets KAI apart. Most security projects install controls and leave. Getting people to actually follow the rules is the hardest part, and it is where KAI focuses.

The order we build in

We start with who can sign in, then build outward.

This is KAI’s recommended order of priority. It starts with identity because almost every other control depends on knowing who, or what, is asking for access.

The numbers show priority, not a rigid gate. We get sign-in and access steady before locking down data broadly. Otherwise you tend to block the wrong things and slow the team down.

  1. IdentityKnow who, and what (including AI tools), is allowed to sign in, and with what level of permission.
  2. AccessDecide who or what can reach which information, and under what conditions.
  3. Device & system trustSet clear expectations for the laptops, phones and connected systems people use.
  4. Risk signalsUse what you learn about sign-ins, access and devices to spot when something looks wrong.
  5. Data protectionIdentify your most important information and protect it, once sign-in and access are steady.
  6. GovernanceAgree who owns what, and set a rhythm of light check-ups so it keeps working over time.

What leaders ask before they start.

What does Zero Trust actually mean? +

It means never assuming someone, or something like an AI tool, should have access just because they had it yesterday. Every request to reach information is checked against clear rules, every time.

How do you protect business data while adopting AI tools? +

Start with who and what can log in and reach your information, including AI tools that act on their own. KAI sets that up first in Microsoft 365 and Entra, then layers on device, data and monitoring controls in a sensible order. AI tools are treated as things that can reach your data, each with a clear owner and limited permissions.

Do we need to replace our current software? +

No. The Foundation is built on Microsoft 365 and Entra, which many growing businesses already pay for. KAI configures what you already have and adds something new only where it clearly earns its place. If your business runs mainly on a different platform, talk to KAI first about fit.

How long does it take? +

A typical engagement runs about eight weeks: a short assessment, then building the baseline, then a rollout window so the team adopts it. The exact scope is agreed in a discovery call.

Can we buy the Foundation without AI Workflows? +

Yes. The KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation is a complete service on its own. Many businesses start here, then add AI Workflows once the foundation is in place.
