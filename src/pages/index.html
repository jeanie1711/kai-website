Why security comes with the AI

AI agents work with the access you give them.

An AI agent reads, searches and acts on company data far faster than a person can. That makes it useful. It also means every gap in access, sharing or ownership shows up sooner.

Oversharing gets found faster

If a folder is open to everyone today, an AI assistant can surface it in seconds. Fixing sharing first keeps answers inside the right boundaries.

Agents need identities too

Each agent should have its own permissions, limited to the data and actions it needs, and reviewed as the workflow changes. That is Zero Trust applied to AI.

Trust drives adoption

People use AI tools they trust. Clear rules about what AI can see and when a person decides make it easier for the whole team to get on board.

KAI offers two services for growing businesses in Southeast Asia. The KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation sets up the security policies, controls and team habits that protect company data, built on Microsoft 365 and Entra.

Can we buy AI Workflows without the Zero Trust Security Foundation? +

Yes. Each service can be bought on its own. If you start with AI Workflows only, KAI still checks the access controls your selected workflow needs before connecting any data. We recommend the Zero Trust Security Foundation alongside it, because an AI agent can only be as safe as the access rules behind it.

Who is KAI for? +

Small and medium-sized businesses across Southeast Asia that want to use AI in everyday operations without losing control of their data, especially teams without a large in-house IT or security function.

No. Start with the process you want to improve. KAI recommends tools based on your current systems, your access requirements and the steps that need automation.
