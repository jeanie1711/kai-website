Use AI with confidence, without losing control.

What we believe

  • Don’t stop AI. Build the right foundation to use it safely.
  • Start with the business outcome, not the tool.
  • A project is only done when the team keeps using it.
The story

Why KAI exists.

Over the last couple of years, AI has quietly become part of everyday work. People use AI helpers to write proposals, sum up documents and put together presentations. It’s genuinely useful.

But here’s the part most business owners miss. AI isn’t just staff using ChatGPT. It’s also the AI features already switched on inside the tools they pay for, and the automations quietly moving information around in the background. All of these can reach company data, often without anyone deciding who’s allowed to, or keeping any record of it.

The problem isn’t AI. The problem is that most businesses have never set the ground rules for it.

That’s why we built KAI. We help growing businesses do two things well: put AI to work on the repetitive tasks that slow their teams down, and put the right security basics in place so that AI only reaches what it should. We start with what the business is trying to achieve, then build around the tools it already has wherever we can.

And we don’t stop once things are switched on. We stay until the team really takes to the new way of working, until it’s just how things are done. That’s the part most others skip, and it’s the part that makes the whole thing last.

Using AI should give a business more confidence, not more worry.

Questions people usually ask.

Is KAI a cybersecurity company? +

Not in the usual sense. KAI builds AI workflows for everyday operations and the security foundation underneath them, and, unlike most, stays until the team actually uses both. That last part is where a lot of technology projects fall down, and it is what KAI is best at.

Does KAI sell AI software? +

No. KAI does not sell its own AI product. It designs and implements AI workflows using tools that fit the systems a business already uses, and sets up the access rules those tools need.

What does getting the team to use it actually mean? +

Helping everyone take to the new way of working through clear communication, simple guidance for each role, a few people inside the team to help lead it, and regular follow-up, so it becomes normal rather than something written down and forgotten.

Isn’t AI risk just people pasting things into ChatGPT? +

That is only part of it. Risk also comes from AI features switched on inside tools you already use, and from automations that move information around on their own. All of them can reach your data, so all of them need a clear owner and clear rules.

Do we need a big IT team to work with KAI? +

No. Small teams are exactly who KAI helps. KAI keeps things simple and practical, and leaves you able to run and look after them yourselves afterwards.

We recently had a near-miss with data. Is that a bad time to talk? +

It’s actually one of the most common reasons people come to us. We help turn that moment into a clear, calm plan, starting with what matters most.
