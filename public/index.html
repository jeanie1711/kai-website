Common questions.

What does KAI do? +

KAI brings AI agents into everyday work for growing businesses in Southeast Asia, with Zero Trust security. KAI designs, builds and helps teams adopt AI workflows for sales outreach, lead nurturing, knowledge search, employee onboarding, and documents and follow-ups. Every agent works with approved data, defined actions and human oversight.

What does Zero Trust security mean for an AI agent? +

The agent only uses agreed data sources and respects existing access permissions, can only read, update or send what you define, and works with named people who approve key steps and own the outcome.

Where should we start? +

With one process your team repeats every week. Many businesses start with sales outreach, where AI researches leads and drafts messages for the sales team to approve. KAI builds a pilot, measures results and helps the team use it before deciding what to scale.

Do we need to secure our whole company first? +

Not necessarily. KAI checks the controls each workflow needs before connecting any data. For company-wide protection, KAI also offers the KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation, built on Microsoft 365 and Entra, which can be bought on its own or alongside AI workflows.

Who is KAI for? +

Growing businesses of roughly 20 to 200 people across Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia and the Philippines that want to use AI in everyday work without losing control of their data.
