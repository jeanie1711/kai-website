Employee Onboarding. Everyone knows what to do.

Employee Onboarding is an AI workflow that prepares a role-specific checklist when a new starter is confirmed, shares approved guides, assigns setup tasks and follows up with the people responsible, so new starters and the colleagues helping them know what to do. Account access still goes through your normal approval.

Employee Onboarding questions.

Does the AI give new hires access to company systems? +

No. The workflow requests access, but granting it still goes through your existing approval process. Access is based on the new hire’s role, so people start with what they need and nothing more.

Can the same workflow handle offboarding? +

Yes, and we recommend it. Removing access promptly when someone leaves is one of the most important Zero Trust habits, and the same checklist approach makes sure no account is forgotten.

Do we need an HR system to start? +

No. The workflow can start from a simple form or an email when a new starter is confirmed. If you have an HR system, KAI can connect to it later.
