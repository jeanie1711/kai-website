AI Workflow

Operations Follow-up. Every agreed action gets an owner.

Operations Follow-up is an AI workflow that reads approved meeting notes and incoming requests, proposes tasks with owners and due dates, and follows up until they are done. A person confirms anything unclear before it is assigned.

Operations Follow-up questions.

Does the AI read all of our email and chats? +

No. It works only with the inputs you agree on, such as approved meeting notes or one shared request inbox. Personal mailboxes and other channels stay outside the workflow.

Which task tool does it use? +

Preferably the one your team already uses. KAI connects the workflow to your existing task or project tool so people do not have to learn another system.

What if the AI picks the wrong owner or deadline? +

The AI only proposes. When an owner or deadline is unclear, a named person confirms it before anything is assigned, and owners can correct a task at any time.
