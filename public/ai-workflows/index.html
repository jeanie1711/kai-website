Before you get started.

Do we need to know which AI tool to use? +

No. Start with the process you want to improve. The tools depend on your current systems, access requirements and the steps that need automation.

Can we buy AI Workflows without the KAI Zero Trust Security Foundation? +

Yes. KAI reviews the controls needed for your selected workflow before connecting any data, and defines any gaps in identity, access or sharing that must be fixed as part of the plan. The full Foundation is recommended alongside AI Workflows because it protects all company data, not only the data one workflow uses.

Will AI make decisions without our approval? +

We agree on that boundary together. Routine steps can be automated, while sensitive decisions, messages to customers and approvals stay with named people. The workflow plan defines approval points, ownership and how errors are handled.

How do we know whether the pilot is useful? +

Choose a measure before building, such as qualified leads and meetings booked for sales, or time spent finding information for a knowledge assistant. Review the pilot against that measure and gather feedback from the people using it.
